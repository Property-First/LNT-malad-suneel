L&T Malad Master Plan

The L&T Malad master plan places residential towers around a central amenity spine, keeping podium-level greens, the clubhouse zone and sports courts well clear of vehicular movement and basement/ground parking. Towers are oriented for daylight and cross-ventilation across units, with dedicated fire-safety refuge areas, wide internal roads and clearly zoned entry/exit points for residents and visitors — the kind of planning detail that matters more once you've moved in than it does on a brochure page.
